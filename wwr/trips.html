Featured Trips

Family Scenic Float

Difficulty: Class I - II (Beginner)

Perfect for families with young children or first-time rafters! Enjoy a relaxed, scenic paddle along gentle currents with plenty of opportunities to swim and spot local wildlife.

Canyon Rush

Difficulty: Class III - IV (Intermediate)

Our most popular trip! Navigate continuous, thrilling rapids carving through dramatic canyon walls. Includes a mid-day riverside lunch cooked fresh by your river guide.

Thunder Gorge Challenge

Difficulty: Class IV - V (Advanced)

An adrenaline-packed full-day expedition through high-velocity rapids. Designed for experienced paddle crews looking for the ultimate river challenge.
